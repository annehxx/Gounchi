Additional Information

Travel

I am happy to travel within Germany for engagements of 6 hours or more, and within Europe for dates of 8 hours or more. All travel-related expenses, including flights, trains, and accommodation, are to be covered by the client.

FMTY (Fly-Me-To-You) bookings to other destinations are available on a case-by-case basis. Please feel free to reach out with your preferred city, and I will be happy to provide further details.

For touring engagements, my rates are to be converted from Euros into the local currency.

Booking Conditions

  • All bookings longer than 3 hours must include a meal outside of the hotel room. Room service may be possible for an additional fee.
  • Overnight bookings require a minimum of 7 hours of uninterrupted sleep.
  • All bookings require a 20% deposit (50% for FMTY engagements).
  • Deposits may be paid via IBAN transfer, BTC, ETH, Wishlist (credit card payment possible), or Interac e-transfer (Canadian accounts only).
  • Dates are not confirmed until the deposit has been received.

Deposit Terms

  • Deposits are fully refundable if cancellation occurs more than 14 days prior to our meeting.
  • Cancellations made between 2 and 14 days in advance may have the deposit credited toward a rescheduled date.
  • Cancellations made less than 48 hours before our date will result in the deposit being retained as a cancellation fee.
  • For FMTY and multi-day engagements, the deposit will be retained as a cancellation fee if cancellation occurs 7 days or less before our scheduled time.
