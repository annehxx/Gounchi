Hi, I’m Cleo.

I was born in Russia and grew up in Germany – a contrast that has shaped the way I see the world and the people in it. My curiosity, my love for depth, and my fascination with human connection have guided me through many paths, both personal and academic. I am currently pursuing my master’s degree with a focus on social sciences and politics, drawn to the visible and invisible structures that influence how we live, desire, and relate to one another.

At the heart of everything I do lies connection – in all its forms. I love meeting people beyond the surface, discovering their worlds, sharing laughter, and creating moments that feel playful, intense, sensual, or quietly profound. Every encounter carries its own energy and potential. Sometimes effortless and light, sometimes electric and deeply stirring. I cherish anticipation: the slow build through conversation, the shared drink or meal, the spark that grows when curiosity meets chemistry. For me, the most memorable experiences are those where mutual understanding unfolds naturally – where intellect, humor, and desire intertwine, and where both of us feel truly seen.

I have a naturally athletic body with soft curves that I know how to accentuate. My fiery red hair frames my face and draws attention to my deep brown eyes. I’ve often been told my skin is softer than silk – but I prefer to let you decide that for yourself. Intelligence and humor are qualities I value deeply, in myself and in others. I enjoy being well-read, staying curious, exploring new places, and finding beauty in both meaningful conversations and life’s small absurdities. The best encounters, to me, are those that feel genuine – an escape not only into physical closeness, but into a shared moment that lingers long after we part.
